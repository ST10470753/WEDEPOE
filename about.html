About Our Organisation

Discover our history, vision, mission, and leadership team.

Our History

Founded in 2018, Hope Horizon started as a local emergency night shelter and expanded into a full transitional community support center.

Our Mission

To provide safe shelter, nutritious food, and empowering resources that assist individuals transitioning into stable, independent living.

Our Vision

A compassionate community where every individual has access to safe housing, personal support, and growth opportunities.
