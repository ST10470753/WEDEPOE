Our Community Services

Detailed overview of key offerings designed for shelter clients and community members.

Temporary Overnight Accommodation

Safe, clean, climate-controlled beds equipped with secure storage, private shower amenities, and basic personal hygiene kits for men and women.

Community Kitchen & Food Parcels

Daily hot meals served three times a day. We also distribute weekly essential food packages to vulnerable local families in need.

Vocational Skills Workshops

Practical practical skill development including resume preparation, basic computer literacy, woodworking, and interview readiness training.

Social Work & Rehabilitation Counselling

On-site professional social workers who assist clients with ID document assistance, addiction recovery support, and social grant processing.

Healthcare Screening & Referral

Bi-weekly health checkups hosted in partnership with mobile health clinics to address immediate medical care requirements.

Transitional Housing Placement

Graduation support helping clients transition from short-term shelter into stable, long-term independent housing solutions.
